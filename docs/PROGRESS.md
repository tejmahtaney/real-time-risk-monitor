- Part 19: the split detector (Part 7) only sees splits inside the 100-day fetched window and only matches ratios within 2 % of 1.5, 2, 3, 4, 5, 10, 20 or their inverses. A real one-day drop of about 33 % would be misread as a 3-for-2 split. It is logged as a warning, so it is visible.
- Part 19: the simulator has one drift μ for every symbol, and the vol shocks of different symbols correlate through the price shocks (corr of W_i, W_j is ρ² times the price correlation). Neither is calibrated from data.
- Part 19: exchange holidays are ignored by `SimClock`, so a simulated session runs on days the real market is shut.
- Part 22: no storage path supports delete yet (the app never deletes on its own, per CLAUDE.md); the storage browser will need one, behind an explicit user-triggered action.

If a later part makes the rate move (a stretch item, not on the roadmap today), `PnlAttributor::on_tick`'s signature will need a rate before/after to compute a real dr.

- Deviations from the prompt, both in the test tolerance only. (1) Each finite-difference tolerance adds the round-off the difference cannot avoid, 8·ε·(S + K)/hⁿ, because a deep in-the-money price is made of terms of size S + K and the fixed 1e-8 floor is smaller than that noise for short-dated, low-vol options. (2) Volga uses one Richardson step, (4·D(h/2) − D(h))/3 with h = 1e-4. A plain h = 1e-4 second difference fails 1e-5 relative error at 5 % vol six deviations out of the money, where the h² truncation term alone is about 6e-5. The analytic value was right.

### Part 04 - Implied volatility solver

- Built: `pricing::implied_volatility` taking an `IvRequest` and returning an `IvSolution` (volatility and iteration count) or an `IvError` (`Expired`, `BelowIntrinsic`, `AboveUpperBound`, `NoConvergence`, `InvalidInput`). Safeguarded Newton on vega with a bracket [1e-6, 10] tightened on every evaluation, bisection when vega is under 1e-12 or the step leaves the bracket, and Brenner-Subrahmanyam as the start guess. Validation reuses `BsInputs::new` with a placeholder σ of 1, and a non-finite price is `PricingError::NonFinite`. One `greeks()` call per iteration gives price and vega.
- New dependencies: none. A second criterion bench, `benches/implied_vol.rs`.
- Tests: 67 in the workspace (51 unit and property tests, 16 doc tests). 13 new unit and property tests, 1 new doc test. Every `IvError` variant has a test. The property tests also passed at 300,000 cases per test.
- Iterations: over a grid of 5,040 round-trip cases the mean is 8.64 and the maximum 33. Pure bisection needs about 50. The 64 limit holds over 300,000 random cases.
- Benchmark (release, this machine): 160 ns for one near-the-money solve and 762 ns for a deep out-of-the-money one.
- Choices the prompt left open. (1) The upper-bound tolerance is one-sided the same way as the lower: a price within 1e-12 × max(S, K) of S·e^(−qT) or K·e^(−rT) counts as on the bound and is `AboveUpperBound`. (2) A root below 1e-6, such as a price exactly on intrinsic, returns 1e-6 and not an error. (3) A root above 10 is `NoConvergence`, detected when the bracket collapses onto the ceiling with the price still out of reach. (4) Where vega is tiny the solver returns any σ that reproduces the price to 1e-12 × max(1, price), which need not be the σ that made it.

### Part 09 - P&L attribution

- Built: `risk::PnlBuckets` (`delta`, `gamma`, `vega`, `theta`, `rho`, `higher_order`, `residual`, `actual`, with `Add`/`AddAssign` and `explained()`), `PnlLevel` (today plus since-start buckets), `PnlReport` and `PreviousDay` for the API (`crates/risk/src/pnl.rs`), and `PnlAttributor` (`crates/risk/src/attributor.rs`), which keeps the previous tick's `PositionRisk` per position id and turns `on_tick(&[PositionRisk], &ClockStep)` calls into running per-position, per-underlying and portfolio totals. ### Part 10 - Limits and alerts

- Built: `config/limits.toml` (validated TOML) and `risk::LimitsConfig` (`crates/risk/src/limits.rs`): `PortfolioLimits` (`dollar_delta`, `dollar_gamma_1pct`, `vega`, `theta_floor`, `gross_exposure`, `max_concentration`), `UnderlyingDeltaLimit` (a `default` plus per-symbol `overrides`), top-level `warning_threshold`/`clear_threshold`/`var_99` (parsed, unused until Part 18), and `LimitsConfigError`. `risk::monitor` (`crates/risk/src/monitor.rs`): `LimitKind`, `Scope` (`Portfolio` or `Underlying(Symbol)`), `LimitState` (`Ok`/`Warning`/`Breach`), `LimitStatus`, `AlertEvent` and `LimitMonitor::evaluate(&RiskSummary, sim_time) -> Vec<AlertEvent>`, which raises an event only on a state change, keeps a map of currently active (non-`Ok`) statuses and a 200-event history ring buffer, and takes a `Box<dyn domain::Clock>` for the wall time stamped on each event (mirroring `HistoryLoader`'s pattern, since `risk` cannot depend on `marketdata`'s `SystemClock`). Hysteresis is exactly the prompt's rule: once `Breach`, a limit stays there until utilisation drops below `clear_threshold`; the `Ok`/`Warning` line has no such protection. The theta floor compares against `max(0, -theta)`, so a book earning theta never breaches it. New example `crates/engine/examples/tune_limits.rs` runs the simulator headless for 10 sessions of seed 42 over the sample portfolio, prints min/p50/p90/p99/max for every limited measure, and reports alert events per session and the time of the first one — used to pick the values in `config/limits.toml`.
- New dependencies: `thiserror` and `toml`, both already in `[workspace.dependencies]`, added to `risk`'s own `[dependencies]` (`thiserror` for `LimitsConfigError`, matching `marketdata::MarketConfig`'s pattern; `toml` to parse `config/limits.toml` directly in `risk` rather than pushing config-loading up into `engine`).
- Tests: 318 in the workspace (up from 304). `risk` gained 14 unit tests: 7 for `LimitsConfig` (loads a valid file, rejects a negative limit, a zero limit, bad thresholds, `warning_threshold >= 1`, `max_concentration` out of `(0, 1]`, and an unknown field) and 7 for `LimitMonitor` (`Ok → Warning → Breach` and back, hysteresis keeping one breach event through several 0.97×/1.01× oscillations, no duplicate event when the state is unchanged, a per-symbol override beating the default, the 200-event history cap, the theta floor ignoring positive theta, and concentration using the largest share), for 36 unit tests total (was 22). `crates/engine/tests/attribution_day.rs` also gained two assertions ahead of this part (a separate commit, "Part 09: Assert settlement and day roll in attribution test"): that `spy-call-expiring` ends the session `Expired`, and that `report().previous_day` is `Some` after the overnight roll — the test count is unchanged since these are assertions inside the existing test, not new tests.
- Tuning `tune_limits`: every risk figure here is a driftless random walk (`simulation.toml`'s `drift = 0.0`), so a warning line placed inside a measure's normal per-session range gets crossed dozens of times by ordinary noise with no hysteresis to stop it (hysteresis in this design only protects the breach line) — an early attempt with round-number limits produced 20–50 alert events in some sessions and 0 in others. The fix was placing each limit either comfortably below the floor a measure actually occupies (crossed once, early, stays crossed) or comfortably above its ceiling (kept in reserve). Final values: `portfolio.dollar_delta = 550,000` is deliberately tight — the sample book's static stock alone carries about $490,000 of dollar delta, so this is in breach from the first tick of every session, the demo's one designed alert (real time 0.2 s in, comfortably inside "the first minute"). ### Part 13 - Local storage and session recording

- Choices the prompt left open. (1) The recorder is the only place in `engine` that performs file I/O — not `Engine::new`, not `Engine::apply`, not `Engine::tick` — so a slow disk can only ever cost a dropped record, never a delayed tick. (2) A settled position's exclusion from the persisted book happens once, in `Engine::active_positions`, and both `current.json` and a saved version read through it, rather than each of the two write paths re-implementing the same filter. (3) The event/history JSONL writers batch and flush once a second regardless of tick rate, rather than flushing every tick, since `config/simulation.toml`'s default tick interval (250 ms) would otherwise mean four small appends a second for no crash-safety benefit beyond what a one-second window already gives. (4) `crates/engine/build.rs` watches the ref file HEAD points at (`refs/heads/<branch>`) in addition to `.git/HEAD` itself, since `.git/HEAD` only changes on a branch switch and a rebuild that never notices a new commit on the same branch would embed a stale hash.
