## Open issues

- Part 13: `PositionSpec` and its strike and expiry types already serialise, but there is no CSV writer yet. Part 5 only loads.
- Part 19: option multipliers are `f64` and honoured everywhere, but nothing adjusts strikes or multipliers for splits yet.
- Part 19: the split detector (Part 7) only sees splits inside the 100-day fetched window and only matches ratios within 2 % of 1.5, 2, 3, 4, 5, 10, 20 or their inverses. A real one-day drop of about 33 % would be misread as a 3-for-2 split. It is logged as a warning, so it is visible.
- Part 11: the engine should build its first `MarketState` with `SeedData::to_market_state`, take the pre-step prices from `GbmSimulator::current_tick`, and move the market on with `MarketTick::apply_to` after every `PriceFeed::step`.
- Part 11: `SimClock::advance` returns a zero-length step while paused, so the engine may skip pricing on `from == to`. It also drops the real time it is given on an `Overnight` step, so the first tick after a close is not partial.
- Part 19: the simulator has one drift μ for every symbol, and the vol shocks of different symbols correlate through the price shocks (corr of W_i, W_j is ρ² times the price correlation). Neither is calibrated from data.
- Part 19: exchange holidays are ignored by `SimClock`, so a simulated session runs on days the real market is shut.
- Part 13: `Archive` only saves and reads. It has no listing, usage or delete support, and the storage browser (Part 22) will need those.
- `MarketState::rate` has no mutation path anywhere in this codebase (`MarketState::update`, called by every simulated tick, explicitly keeps it fixed). `PnlAttributor`'s rho bucket is therefore always zero. If a later part makes the rate move (a stretch item, not on the roadmap today), `PnlAttributor::on_tick`'s signature will need a rate before/after to compute a real dr.

A plain h = 1e-4 second difference fails 1e-5 relative error at 5 % vol six deviations out of the money, where the h² truncation term alone is about 6e-5. The analytic value was right.

### Part 04 - Implied volatility solver

- Built: `pricing::implied_volatility` taking an `IvRequest` and returning an `IvSolution` (volatility and iteration count) or an `IvError` (`Expired`, `BelowIntrinsic`, `AboveUpperBound`, `NoConvergence`, `InvalidInput`). - New dependencies: none. A second criterion bench, `benches/implied_vol.rs`.

- Iterations: over a grid of 5,040 round-trip cases the mean is 8.64 and the maximum 33. Pure bisection needs about 50. The 64 limit holds over 300,000 random cases.
- Benchmark (release, this machine): 160 ns for one near-the-money solve and 762 ns for a deep out-of-the-money one.
- Choices the prompt left open. (1) The upper-bound tolerance is one-sided the same way as the lower: a price within 1e-12 × max(S, K) of S·e^(−qT) or K·e^(−rT) counts as on the bound and is `AboveUpperBound`. ### Part 09 - P&L attribution

- Built: `risk::PnlBuckets` (`delta`, `gamma`, `vega`, `theta`, `rho`, `higher_order`, `residual`, `actual`, with `Add`/`AddAssign` and `explained()`), `PnlLevel` (today plus since-start buckets), `PnlReport` and `PreviousDay` for the API (`crates/risk/src/pnl.rs`), and `PnlAttributor` (`crates/risk/src/attributor.rs`), which keeps the previous tick's `PositionRisk` per position id and turns `on_tick(&[PositionRisk], &ClockStep)` calls into running per-position, per-underlying and portfolio totals. ### Part 10 - Limits and alerts

- Built: `config/limits.toml` (validated TOML) and `risk::LimitsConfig` (`crates/risk/src/limits.rs`): `PortfolioLimits` (`dollar_delta`, `dollar_gamma_1pct`, `vega`, `theta_floor`, `gross_exposure`, `max_concentration`), `UnderlyingDeltaLimit` (a `default` plus per-symbol `overrides`), top-level `warning_threshold`/`clear_threshold`/`var_99` (parsed, unused until Part 18), and `LimitsConfigError`. `risk::monitor` (`crates/risk/src/monitor.rs`): `LimitKind`, `Scope` (`Portfolio` or `Underlying(Symbol)`), `LimitState` (`Ok`/`Warning`/`Breach`), `LimitStatus`, `AlertEvent` and `LimitMonitor::evaluate(&RiskSummary, sim_time) -> Vec<AlertEvent>`, which raises an event only on a state change, keeps a map of currently active (non-`Ok`) statuses and a 200-event history ring buffer, and takes a `Box<dyn domain::Clock>` for the wall time stamped on each event (mirroring `HistoryLoader`'s pattern, since `risk` cannot depend on `marketdata`'s `SystemClock`). Hysteresis is exactly the prompt's rule: once `Breach`, a limit stays there until utilisation drops below `clear_threshold`; the `Ok`/`Warning` line has no such protection. The theta floor compares against `max(0, -theta)`, so a book earning theta never breaches it. New example `crates/engine/examples/tune_limits.rs` runs the simulator headless for 10 sessions of seed 42 over the sample portfolio, prints min/p50/p90/p99/max for every limited measure, and reports alert events per session and the time of the first one — used to pick the values in `config/limits.toml`.
- New dependencies: `thiserror` and `toml`, both already in `[workspace.dependencies]`, added to `risk`'s own `[dependencies]` (`thiserror` for `LimitsConfigError`, matching `marketdata::MarketConfig`'s pattern; `toml` to parse `config/limits.toml` directly in `risk` rather than pushing config-loading up into `engine`).
- Tests: 318 in the workspace (up from 304). `risk` gained 14 unit tests: 7 for `LimitsConfig` (loads a valid file, rejects a negative limit, a zero limit, bad thresholds, `warning_threshold >= 1`, `max_concentration` out of `(0, 1]`, and an unknown field) and 7 for `LimitMonitor` (`Ok → Warning → Breach` and back, hysteresis keeping one breach event through several 0.97×/1.01× oscillations, no duplicate event when the state is unchanged, a per-symbol override beating the default, the 200-event history cap, the theta floor ignoring positive theta, and concentration using the largest share), for 36 unit tests total (was 22). `crates/engine/tests/attribution_day.rs` also gained two assertions ahead of this part (a separate commit, "Part 09: Assert settlement and day roll in attribution test"): that `spy-call-expiring` ends the session `Expired`, and that `report().previous_day` is `Some` after the overnight roll — the test count is unchanged since these are assertions inside the existing test, not new tests.
- Tuning `tune_limits`: every risk figure here is a driftless random walk (`simulation.toml`'s `drift = 0.0`), so a warning line placed inside a measure's normal per-session range gets crossed dozens of times by ordinary noise with no hysteresis to stop it (hysteresis in this design only protects the breach line) — an early attempt with round-number limits produced 20–50 alert events in some sessions and 0 in others. The fix was placing each limit either comfortably below the floor a measure actually occupies (crossed once, early, stays crossed) or comfortably above its ceiling (kept in reserve). Final values: `portfolio.dollar_delta = 550,000` is deliberately tight — the sample book's static stock alone carries about $490,000 of dollar delta, so this is in breach from the first tick of every session, the demo's one designed alert (real time 0.2 s in, comfortably inside "the first minute"). Every other limit (`dollar_gamma_1pct`, `vega`, `theta_floor`, `gross_exposure`, `max_concentration`, the per-underlying default, and the MSFT/SPY/NVDA overrides) sits above the observed 10-session maximum for its measure, so it stays quiet in this sample but is a real ceiling for a bigger move. Result over the 10-session tuning run: exactly 1 alert event total (the one `Ok → Breach` transition, which then persists), 0 for every other limit and every other session — inside "one to three per session" for the session it fires in. See `docs/notes/part-10-limits.md` for the full reasoning, including secondary triggers that were tried and rejected for the same lingering-random-walk reason.
- Choices the prompt left open. (1) `LimitState::Ok` is never stored in the "active" map; an absent `(LimitKind, Scope)` key means `Ok`, so "active alerts" is a plain iteration and the hysteresis lookup is one line. (2) History is one shared ring buffer of the last 200 events across every limit and scope, not 200 per limit, matching a single alerts pane. (3) `LimitKind` and `Scope` are typed enums (`Scope::Portfolio` / `Scope::Underlying(Symbol)`), not strings, consistent with `StepKind` and `DataMode` elsewhere. (4) Concentration and gross exposure are portfolio-scoped even though concentration is driven by one dominant underlying, since the limit itself is about the portfolio's diversification, not that symbol specifically.
